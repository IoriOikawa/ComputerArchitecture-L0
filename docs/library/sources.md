# Sources and coverage

## Attribution

The main chapter PDFs identify **Fengkai Wang (Iori Oikawa), Drew University, Fall 2025**. Many lectures and assignments in the earlier collection identify **Andy Siyuan Feng**. Original attribution and source notices remain in the files. A file's folder year and its internal year may differ; “earlier collection” refers to the `Past Material - 24` source folder.

This index does not relicense third-party teaching materials under the website code license. Preserve the individual files' authorship and notices when adapting them.

## What is included

- All 11 Fall25 chapter PDF readings, including Chapter 5+ and the distinct Chapter 5 alternate.
- The earlier lecture PDFs and four C-topic decks available only as PPTX.
- Identified teaching assignments, classroom exercises, project briefs, original solutions and selected examples.
- A recursion assignment available only as LaTeX source, explicitly labeled as a draft.

The downloadable files are unmodified copies. The repository's `materials/catalog.json` records the original relative path and SHA-256 for each file; `materials/source-inventory.json` records teaching-source variants and their disposition.

## What still needs reconciliation

Individual Fall25 `Lecture N.key` files and other Keynote originals remain in the original course folder. This pass used the grouped chapter PDFs as the reading edition. It did not extract every Keynote slide or establish that each lecture draft is fully represented by its chapter PDF. Editable PPTX counterparts of existing PDFs are recorded as sources, without assuming that they are identical.

Other Assignment 0 and C-project exports remain recorded as alternatives. Mixed student-submission folders, grades, exams, installers and photographs are outside this import. Third-party reference excerpts in the assignment folder have not been promoted to authored course chapters.

## Using the archive

The chapter pages provide topic guidance, page ranges and connections. Search indexes those guide pages; it does not index the full PDF/PPTX text. Historical deadlines and classroom platforms remain visible inside the preserved originals. Known corrections are collected on the [errata page](errata.md).
